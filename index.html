Karhutla Response Timeline

Key monitoring and response milestones

10 August 2026

National priority response reinforced

BNPB highlighted six priority provinces as the government prepared for elevated August–September karhutla risk.

7 September 2026

BMKG flagged critical conditions

Dry conditions, very strong El Niño influence and high hotspot activity remained important risk factors.

16–21 September 2026

Hotspot concentration & air-quality pressure

BMKG and BNPB reported high hotspot concentration in Kalimantan and Sumatra, with smoke and visibility impacts.

28–29 September 2026

Latest six-province response update

BNPB reported 527.6 ha burned in the latest situation snapshot, 431.53 ha handled and 96.07 ha still unextinguished.
